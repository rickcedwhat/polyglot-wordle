Word buckets

Jev answers every question about a word at once. Each card is one independent tree, and every word lands in exactly one leaf of every tree, following Jev's pick. "Unsure" (amber) means Jev gave its pick under that question's threshold (60% by default, 65% for "natives come across it?"); those words go to the Gemini tie-breaker when it could change the fate. "Real word?", "Makes sense?" and the missing-meaning flag only count Jev's "no" at 80%, so they are never unsure. The Fate card shows the final result, including tie-breaks and manual overrides. Hover a box for a preview; click it to keep the popover open, then click words to trace them, or filter every tree to that leaf (same tree: "or"; across trees: "and").
